# Connected Healthcare
The Connected Healthcare Platform is a digital healthcare ecosystem designed to improve how patients and hospitals access healthcare services, manage medical information, and interact with healthcare providers.


# Overview
The platform aims to connect patients, hospitals, doctors, and other healthcare professionals through a centralised digital environment where medical records can be securely accessed and shared, healthcare services can be discovered, consultations can be facilitated, and hospitals can coordinate patient care and resource availability.
The platform will also explore the use of artificial intelligence to help patients understand their symptoms, navigate available healthcare services, and connect with appropriate medical professionals.
Rather than functioning as an isolated hospital management system, the platform aims to establish an interconnected healthcare network that makes healthcare information and services more accessible across participating healthcare institutions.

# The Problem
Healthcare systems, especially in Nigeria, face several interconnected challenges that affect both patients and healthcare providers. The platform seeks to investigate and address the following problems.
  1. Fragmented Patient Medical Records
  2. Difficulty Accessing Healthcare Professionals
  3. Limited Access to Initial Medical Guidance
  4. Poor Coordination Between Healthcare Institutions
  5. Limited Visibility Into Hospital Services

# My Solution
This platform will provide a centralized digital environment connecting patients and participating healthcare institutions.
Its major capabilities will include:
  1. Digital patient medical records.
  2. AI-assisted health assessment and navigation.
  3. Doctor discovery and consultation.
  4. Hospital-to-hospital coordination.
  5. Healthcare facility and service discovery
These capabilities will operate as interconnected modules rather than independent applications.

# Core Features
# Digital Patient Medical Records
A centralized patient-facing medical record system that receives and organizes clinical information contributed by participating healthcare institutions.
Potential capabilities:
  1. Patient registration and identity verification.
  2. Digital patient profiles.
  3. Medical history.
  4. Diagnoses and treatment records.
  5. Prescriptions.
  6. Laboratory results.
  7. Imaging reports.
  8. Allergies and medical conditions.
  9. Hospital visit history.
  10. Medical record sharing.
  11. Patient consent management.
  12. Access history and audit logs.

# AI-Assisted Health Assessment
An interactive AI-powered health navigation experience.
Potential capabilities:
  1. Text-based symptom reporting.
  2. Voice-based interaction.
  3. Guided symptom questions.
  4. Interactive body illustrations.
  5. Audio and visual demonstrations.
  6. Symptom categorisation.
  7. Urgency assessment.
  8. General health education.
  9. Recommendations for appropriate healthcare services.
  10. Hospital and specialist recommendations.
  11. Escalation to human medical professionals.

# Hospital Coordination Network
A system that enables participating hospitals to coordinate patient referrals and transfers.
Potential capabilities:
  1. Hospital profiles.
  2. Hospital service listings.
  3. Bed capacity management.
  4. Emergency capacity reporting.
  5. Specialist availability.
  6. Referral requests.
  7. Hospital-to-hospital communication.
  8. Patient transfer coordination.
  9. Referral acceptance and confirmation.

# Healthcare Discovery
A searchable directory of participating healthcare institutions and services.
Potential capabilities:
  1. Hospital discovery.
  2. Location-based search.
  3. Medical specialty filters.
  4. Available services.
  5. Hospital contact information.
  6. Emergency service information.
  7. Hospital profiles and verification status.

# Future Enhancements
  1. AI-assisted symptom assessment and healthcare guidance.
  2. Intelligent healthcare recommendations.
  3. Patient-doctor communication.
  4. Advanced healthcare analytics.
  5. Interoperability with external healthcare systems.
